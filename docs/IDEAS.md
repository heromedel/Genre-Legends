# Genre Legends — Ideas

(Note) Ideas saved to look at later, not planned into a build. Each is tagged like the design doc (Design Doc
Labelling Guide): **Proposed** is heromedel's idea, **Ai-Generated-Proposal** is Claude's. When one is picked up it
moves into `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` or `docs/ROADMAP.md` and is struck off here.

## Frames and parts

- **Proposed (2026-11-11):** Loadout slots named for where parts mount on the body, Armored Core style, instead of
  "weapon" and "support": Frame, Plating, Core (or Generator), Left Arm, Right Arm, Left Shoulder, Right Shoulder,
  Head, Back. Legs, or the leg type (bipedal, wheels and so on), could be part of the frame. Add more slots for a
  spot if it needs them. Parts go where they'd really mount: homing mini missiles and most homing weapons belong on
  a shoulder or the back, not in a hand as the primary aimed weapon. Go over later: which slots to add, rename or
  remove. (heromedel, after Drone Pods were moved into a weapon slot and pushed out the homing Swarm Pods.)

- **Proposed (2026-11-09):** More frames, each built around a built-in part: a built-in Arc Blade, a built-in Shield
  Generator, built-in shoulder mini missiles. Each pays for its part in Load, Output or Armor (spec 21E, 13S).
- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** Frame names with intent for those three: the **Duelist** (built-in Arc
  Blade, light and fast, low Load), the **Aegis** (built-in Shield Generator, heavy, low Output), the **Salvo** (built-in
  shoulder missiles, high Load, low Armor).
- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** A frame's tier picks could add a fourth, frame-specific pick at tier 3
  (the Tempest: afterimages fire twice; the Bulwark: Fortress Field throws back a third).

## From the 0.6 design review (2026-11-11)

- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** Forks set a tech's element (Splitfire turns Kinetic, Breaker Pulse turns
  Explosive), so a fork is also a Resonance direction. Today Resonance follows the slot layout: Kinetic and Explosive
  have no main weapon or Generator part, and three of the four element-tagged field cards are Kinetic. Make Tractor
  Beam neutral at the same time.
- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** An R&D focus: pick one tech, and half of every replay's blueprints and its
  elite drops go to it (Derelict Dives lean on it too), so the Mk 3 needed for a fusion isn't 15+ replays of two
  particular planets.
- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** Level 5 of each fork becomes a visible behaviour change, the way Chain
  Bolts gains a third jump, instead of a stat line ("Faster rails", "Heavier rail"). About ten techs (GDD 1C).

## Salvage Crates

- **Proposed (2026-11-11):** The crate as a high-tech lock taking its combination, as an alternative to the slot
  reels. (The reels became rolling drums in 0.6.1; the lock is kept here in case the reels ever feel too casino.)
- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** A crate can be "aimed": hold a card from your last level-up and the crate
  favours its tech. Only if the build-deepening crates (GDD 11Y) still feel random in play.

## Supply Depot (mock mobile layer, GDD 8B, 8E)

- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** Derelict Dives that sometimes find something odd instead of loot: a
  distress beacon (a special planet appears on the map), a stowaway (a one-mission companion drone), a Chulak relic
  shard.
- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** A weekly **Signal Challenge**: one fixed planet and loadout for everyone
  that week, scored, paying crystals by how far you get. (The old roadmap's "Daily Signal", made weekly so it isn't
  a chore.)
- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** A **Reward Path**: a long track of crystals, Frame Blueprints and
  free Derelict Dives that fills with missions flown, won or lost, like a battle pass with no premium track.
- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** A tongue-in-cheek "store" that sells nothing: offers like "Mega Crystal
  Bundle: 100% off, forever" whose button explains crystals can only be earned (GDD 8A).

## Things to collect

(Note) Today there are tech blueprints, Frame Blueprints and Mawjig Crystals, plus milestones. Anything new should
have more than one use (Game Design Philosophy: an ingredient with one use has no point).

- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** **Chulak relic shards** from rare derelicts, gate bosses and themed
  branches. Five open the first realm (GDD 10E, ROADMAP Plan G), and spare shards can raise a tech one step past Mk 3
  ("Relic-tuned").
- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** **Boss trophies:** the first win against each boss mounts its trophy in
  the Hangar and unlocks a Rematch in Records (fight that boss alone for crystals), so trophies are both a record and
  a way to farm a boss's plate.
- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** **Data cores** from Research Vessels and secret rooms: lore entries for the
  Guide, and each full set of three fills a gap in a planet card (hidden mini-boss, exact special condition).
- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** **Mawjig Cores** as a second, rarer currency only bosses drop: buy a
  fourth frame tier, re-forge a plate's level effect, or re-roll a planet's reward.

## Missions and the map

- **Proposed (2026-11-09):** Real environmental hazards: meteor swarms and ion storms that knock equipment offline
  (ROADMAP Plan H). Ion storms would pair well with heat: a pulse could spike your frame's heat instead.
- **Ai-Generated-Proposal (2026-11-11):** Planets that reward a build: a "high-heat" planet where vents are clean,
  or a "salvage field" where crates are common but every one is guarded.
